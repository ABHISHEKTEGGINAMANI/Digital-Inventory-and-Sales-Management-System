Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

ApexFlow is an inventory management and point-of-sale billing system. This policy explains what data we handle, how we handle it, and your rights.

1. Data Storage

All business data you enter into ApexFlow (products, sales, customers, settings) is stored exclusively in your web browser's LocalStorage. We do not transmit, upload, or sync your data to any external server, cloud service, or third-party platform.

Your data stays on your device. If you clear your browser data or switch browsers, your ApexFlow data will not carry over unless you manually export and import a backup.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal information, analytics data, usage telemetry, or tracking information of any kind. ApexFlow does not use cookies for tracking purposes.

If you are using ApexFlow through a hosted provider, that provider's privacy policy may also apply to your use of their hosting infrastructure.

3. Third-Party Services

ApexFlow loads the following external resources:

  • Google Fonts (Inter, Outfit, JetBrains Mono) for typography. Google's privacy policy applies to font loading requests.

No other third-party scripts, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking pixels are loaded.

4. Data You Enter

You may enter the following types of data into ApexFlow:

  • Product names, SKUs, barcodes, prices, and stock quantities
  • Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, and purchase history
  • Business information (store name, address, tax ID)
  • Sales transaction records

This data is stored locally and is your responsibility to protect. We recommend regular JSON backups through the Settings page.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any data to third parties. Since data is stored only in your browser, we do not have access to it.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all ApexFlow data at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset Demo Data" function in Settings
  • Clearing your browser's LocalStorage for the ApexFlow domain
  • Clearing all browser data through your browser's settings

7. Security

Because data is stored locally in your browser, security depends on your device and browser configuration. We recommend:

  • Using a device with a password or biometric lock
  • Keeping your browser updated
  • Not using ApexFlow on shared or public computers for sensitive business data

8. Children's Privacy

ApexFlow is a business tool and is not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly handle data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Continued use of ApexFlow after changes constitutes acceptance.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the site operator through the contact information provided in ApexFlow's Store Settings.
